How It Works

Start your league in minutes

1

Create or join a league

Invite your friends with a join code. The person who creates the league becomes the commissioner.

2

Draft your queens

Pick your queens in order before the season starts. Choose wisely — you're locked in once the season begins.

3

Score each episode

The commissioner enters results after each episode. Points are calculated automatically based on performance.

4

Crown your champion

The player with the most points at the end of the season wins the league. Bragging rights included.

🍸 Bar Mode

Built for viewing parties

Watching a new episode with friends? Bar Mode turns any viewing party into a live fantasy competition — no accounts, no setup, just scan and play.
