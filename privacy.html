Privacy Policy

Fantasy Drag  ·  Last Updated: April 29, 2026

Fantasy Drag ("App," "we," "our," or "us") is operated by Ashley Zelinskie. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Fantasy Drag mobile application.

By using Fantasy Drag, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: Email address and display name when you create an account
  • Profile content: Profile photos and team photos you choose to upload
  • League data: League names, team names, draft picks, and score entries you create within the app

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: How you interact with the app (screens visited, features used)
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Push notification tokens: If you enable notifications, we store a device token to deliver them

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your precise location
  • We do not access your contacts
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • We do not collect payment information (the app is free)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Run the fantasy league features (drafts, scoring, leaderboards)
  • Send episode reminder notifications (only if you opt in)
  • Generate AI-powered features (team analysis, episode predictions) — your data is processed by our AI service but is not stored or used to train models
  • Improve the app and fix bugs
  • Respond to support requests

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We share information only in these limited circumstances:

Within the app: Your display name, team name, and league standings are visible to other members of your leagues by design.

Service providers: We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase (database and authentication) — supabase.com/privacy
  • Anthropic (AI features) — anthropic.com/privacy
  • Expo / EAS (app infrastructure) — expo.dev/privacy

Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and league data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us. We will delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (TLS), authentication via Supabase Auth, and row-level security policies. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Fantasy Drag is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below.

California residents (CCPA): You have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of its sale (we do not sell personal information).

EU/UK residents (GDPR): Our legal basis for processing your data is the performance of a contract. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and, where appropriate, through an in-app notification.
